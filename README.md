# Bank Customer Churn Analysis
---
## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on bank customer data to understand customer behavior and identify factors associated with customer churn.
---
## 🎯 Objective

The main objective is to analyze customer characteristics such as:

- Credit Score
- Age
- Geography
- Balance
- Number of Products
- Credit Card
- Active Membership
- Estimated Salary

and understand their relationship with customer churn.
---

The analysis aims to identify customer segments that may have a higher likelihood of churn and provide insights that can support customer retention strategies.
